## Aero7 replacement artwork

The following runtime Microsoft/Windows product-logo images are replaced with
project-owner-supplied Aero7 artwork:

- SevenStart orb sprites;
- Plasma splash, logout, and lock-screen branding;
- all SDDM branding variants; and
- the About/System Information logo.

The replacement artwork was supplied by the Aero7 project owner on 2026-08-03
and is approved by the project owner for use and redistribution in Aero7.

Upstream screenshots and preview images are intentionally retained unchanged
until the release screenshot pass. Non-logo theme resources are also retained
unchanged under the upstream notices. This downstream logo replacement does not
claim that every other visual resource is original Aero7 artwork or that the
upstream Microsoft-asset notice no longer applies.
